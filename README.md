# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Short Description

An interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard built with Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, orders, shipping, customer segments, regions, payment modes, and product performance, with a **15-day sales forecasting** feature.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Excel / CSV**
* **Data Visualization & Forecasting**

## 📂 Data Source

**Super Store Sales Dataset** containing information about orders, sales, profit, products, categories, customers, regions, shipping modes, and payment methods.

## ✨ Features & Highlights

* **Business Problem:** Businesses need a centralized view of sales and profit performance across products, regions, customers, and shipping methods.
* **Dashboard Goal:** Monitor key sales KPIs, identify sales and profit patterns, compare regional and category performance, and support sales planning using forecasting.
* **Key Visuals:** KPIs for **Sales (1.57M), Orders (22K), Profit (175K), and Ship Days (10)**, along with sales by category, sub-category, month, state, segment, region, payment mode, and ship mode. - **Key Insights:** Technology generates the highest category sales at **0.64M**, while Phones lead sub-category sales at **0.20M**. The West region contributes **33%** of sales, and COD accounts for **43%** of payment modes. - **Sales Forecasting:** Includes a **15-day sales forecast** to analyze expected near-term sales trends.
* **Business Impact:** Helps stakeholders monitor sales and profitability, identify high-performing products and regions, understand customer and payment patterns, and support short-term sales planning.
